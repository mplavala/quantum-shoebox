# Video 1 — Project Declaration

**Title:** *I'm a Mathematician, So I'm Building a Qubit – In a Shoebox*
**Link:** https://youtu.be/8iLudTZ9914

## Summary

Project declaration for Quantum Shoebox: a DIY, open-source, room-temperature NV ensemble in diamond, housed in a cardboard shoebox. This video introduces the concept of nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers as effective qubits, initialized with green light, rotated with microwaves, read out via red fluorescence, and lays out the goals: see the Zeeman splitting first, try for a controllable qubit as the stretch goal, learn quantum hardware by building it, document the process, openly release all designs, and make hands-on quantum experimentation accessible outside large laboratories. No hardware yet; parts are on order.

None. Project planning phase.
